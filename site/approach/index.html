Approach

Discover. Design. Build. Improve.

A clear process with tangible outputs at every step. We would rather set realistic expectations early than promise speed we cannot responsibly deliver.

The process

Four steps, each with something to show for it.

Steps can overlap and repeat — real projects rarely move in a straight line. What stays constant is that each step ends with an output you can review.

  1. Step 01

    Discover

    Understand the problem, the people involved and the constraints before proposing a solution.

    What happens

    • Conversations with the people who own and use the process or product
    • Review of existing tools, data and documentation
    • Agreement on goals, constraints and what success looks like

    Outputs

    • A written problem statement and scope
    • Key risks and open questions
    • An estimate for the next phase

    What to expect

    Discovery can be short for well-understood problems and longer for complex ones. Estimates are given after this step, not before it.

  2. Step 02

    Design

    Shape the solution — workflows, interfaces and architecture — and test it before committing to build.

    What happens

    • Workflow, interface and system design
    • Prototypes for the riskiest or least certain parts
    • Review sessions with you and, where possible, with users

    Outputs

    • Flows, wireframes or prototypes
    • A technical approach and agreed scope for the build

    What to expect

    Designs often change after feedback. That is the point of this step — it is cheaper to change a design than finished software.

  3. Step 03

    Build

    Develop in small, reviewable increments so progress is visible and course corrections are cheap.

    What happens

    • Iterative development with regular check-ins
    • Testing throughout, not only at the end
    • Deployment to an agreed environment

    Outputs

    • Working software or automations
    • Documentation and hand-over notes

    What to expect

    Timelines depend on scope and complexity. If something changes, we will tell you early and agree how to adjust scope, time or budget.

  4. Step 04

    Improve

    Measure how the solution performs in real use and refine it based on evidence.

    What happens

    • Review of usage, feedback and issues after launch
    • Prioritised fixes and improvements
    • Recommendations for what to do next — including when to stop

    Outputs

    • A short review of what is working and what is not
    • A prioritised list of next steps

    What to expect

    Real-world use always reveals something new. Planning for a period of refinement leads to better outcomes than treating launch as the finish line.

Working principles

How we keep projects honest.

  • Clarity before code

    We agree the problem, scope and success criteria in writing before anything is built.

  • Honest estimates

    Estimates come after discovery, and we flag changes as soon as we see them.

  • Small, visible steps

    Work is delivered in increments you can review, so there are no surprises at the end.

  • Built to hand over

    Documentation and clear structure, so your team can understand and extend what we build.
