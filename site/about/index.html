About

A founder-led technology business.

JP & Co. designs intelligent products, streamlines complex workflows and builds software — with a long-term aim of creating our own B2B SaaS products.

Mission

To build technology that is useful, well made, and honest about what it can do.

We focus on the problems that matter to the business — then choose the simplest approach that solves them well, whether that is an automation, a better-designed product or new software.

Direction

From services to products.

Today, JP & Co. works with businesses on AI and workflow automation, product design, software engineering and product strategy.

Over time, we plan to develop proprietary B2B SaaS products, informed by the problems we see in that work. Our services and our products share the same standard: clear purpose, careful design and solid engineering.

Values

What we hold ourselves to.

  • Clarity

    Plain language, clear scope and decisions that are written down.

  • Craft

    Careful design and engineering, built to last beyond launch.

  • Integrity

    No inflated claims. We say what we can do, and what we cannot.

  • Curiosity

    Understanding the problem deeply before deciding on the solution.
